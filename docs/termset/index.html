Are you tired of terminal clutter? Managing 10-20 tabs of coding agents? Want something tidier?

Here's a terminal I built and have used for 10mo. It runs on macOS and Linux. It's native and doesn't waste screen space or CPU and doesn't have a billion features, it just has one - tidy terminal workspaces.

Tabs come with human names and go into sections. You can make new tabs, close them, reorder them.

You can navigate up/down the tabs using [Ctrl+Shift | Cmd] + Up/Down.

Your workspace is a persistent config in ~/.terms/workspace01.yaml. You can commit it, open it on another machine.

Links are supported, one-click to open a web URL or a filesystem location. You can even right-click and "Search with Google" within the terminal.

There's also a jump-to-bottom of terminal button. In case you hate scrolling.

This is my terminal emulator. It's extremely simple and functional and it is not going to have any more features or pivoting to like raise more money from VC's or whatever. I use it everyday and I love it.
